# Ummah One — Local Contributors: Brief and Agreement

DRAFT for Bushra to check · 13 Nov 2026 · Not legal advice: have a lawyer check section 6 before use

## 1. What and why

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Goal | Real photos, short videos and local tips for each of the 81 cities, taken by people who **live there**. Credited by name, with no image-rights problems. |
| Who | Local residents of the 81 cities: students (for the university cities), local guides, artisans and shopkeepers' family members, retired locals |
| Pay | Up to **10 AED an hour** (estimate). **To verify:** this may be below the minimum wage in some countries (for example Türkiye, Malaysia, Montenegro). Check each country before offering. |
| Spirit | Sadaqah jariyah: helping travellers, students and local businesses for years after the work is done |
| How many | Start with 1–2 contributors in 10 pilot cities, then all 81 (estimate) |

## 2. What each contributor collects (one city)

| Item | How many | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Market photos | 10 | Souqs, bazaars, craft streets; shop name and street in the caption |
| Best things to buy | 10 | One photo per item, with shop name, area and a price range if known |
| Landmarks | 5 | The city's main sights, from outside, in daylight |
| Heritage places | 5 | Old city, palaces, monuments: only where photos are allowed |
| Short videos | 3 | Up to 60 seconds; walk through a market or street. **No background music** (copyright); natural sound only |
| Local tips | 1 page | 300 words: what to buy and where, best time to go, prices to expect, what tourists get wrong |
| Check our guide | 1 | Read the city's Ummah One entry and mark what is wrong or out of date |

## 3. Photo and video rules

- Take everything yourself, in 2026–2027, with your own phone. Never use photos from the internet or from other people.
- Send the **original files**: no filters, no text, no watermarks, no AI editing. Location and date on if possible.
- Landscape for videos; photos at the phone's full size.
- **People:** ask before photographing anyone. A recognisable face needs a signed consent form (section 7). No children's faces without a parent's consent. Shopkeepers: ask before photographing their shop and goods.
- **Places of worship:** outside only, unless staff give permission. No photos during prayer.
- **Halal:** no bars, alcohol, pork, nightclubs or haram goods in any photo.
- **Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait:** no music, concerts or performances.
- Respect "no photo" signs, museums' rules and military or government buildings (often banned).
- No drones unless legal there and you hold the permit.

## 4. How it works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Apply | Short form: name, city, how long you have lived there, phone (OTP check), 3 sample photos |
| 2. Trial | One paid hour: 5 market photos and 3 buys with captions |
| 3. Agree | Sign the agreement (section 6) and the photo consent forms you need |
| 4. Collect | Full city list (section 2), within 2–3 weeks (estimate) |
| 5. Review | The Ummah One team checks every file. Bad or unsafe files are returned, not used |
| 6. Pay | After review, for the approved hours |
| 7. Credit | Your name (or chosen display name) shows on each photo and tip, with a badge and level in the app |

Nothing goes into the app until the team has reviewed it. The team has the final say on what is used.

## 5. Captions (one line per file)

**File name:** city-topic-number, e.g. `konya-market-03.jpg`

**Caption:** What it is · Place (shop or street) · Area · Date · Your name

Example: *Hand-knotted carpets · Bedesten Bazaar, shop 12 · Konya centre · 14 Jan 2027 · Ayşe K.*
