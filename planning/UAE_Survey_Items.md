# Ummah One — UAE Survey Items

The questions testers answer for the 3 UAE cities. Each part also has a "What's missing?" box, and each city has a free-writing box at the end.

## Dubai

**1. Things to do** (Correct / Wrong / Not open / Don't know)

1. Visit the Burj Khalifa and enjoy the panoramic view from the observation deck.
2. Watch the Dubai Fountain show dance across Burj Lake after sunset.
3. Explore the Dubai Mall, one of the largest shopping centres in the world, with its aquarium and endless food court.
4. Take a traditional abra boat ride across Dubai Creek.
5. Wander the wind-tower lanes of Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood and visit Al Fahidi Fort, Dubai's oldest building.
6. Browse the Gold Souk, Spice Souk, Textile Souk, and Perfume Souk in Deira and Bur Dubai.
7. Go on a desert safari for dune bashing, camel rides, and dinner under the stars.
8. Relax on Jumeirah Beach, Kite Beach, or La Mer with the Burj Al Arab on the horizon.
9. Drive out along the Palm Jumeirah and see the landmark Atlantis The Royal.
10. Enjoy dinner by the water in Dubai Marina as the towers light up.
11. Step inside the Museum of the Future, as striking outside as it is within.
12. Visit Global Village for food, crafts, and pavilions from dozens of countries (open roughly October to May).
13. Escape to Hatta for kayaking on the turquoise Hatta Dam and cooler air in the Hajar Mountains.
14. Stand on the glass floor of the Dubai Frame in Zabeel Park, with old Dubai on one side and new Dubai on the other.
15. See the city from above on a helicopter tour or seaplane flight.
16. Walk through a living rainforest at The Green Planet in City Walk.
17. Ski or meet the penguins at Ski Dubai inside Mall of the Emirates.
18. Eat your way around the world—Lebanese mezze, Pakistani karahi, Japanese ramen, Thai curries—almost all of it halal.
19. Take a sunset dhow cruise along the Creek or the Marina with dinner on board.

## Abu Dhabi

**1. Things to do** (Correct / Wrong / Not open / Don't know)

1. Visit the Sheikh Zayed Grand Masjid and see its white marble glow at sunset.
2. Explore the Louvre Abu Dhabi beneath its vast, light-filtering dome.
3. Tour Qasr Al Watan, the working presidential palace, and stay for the evening light show.
4. Step into history at Qasr Al Hosn, the city's oldest stone building.
5. Walk or cycle the Corniche from Marina Mall towards the Heritage Village.
6. Ride the world's fastest roller coaster at Ferrari World on Yas Island.
7. Spend a day at Warner Bros. World or Yas Waterworld on Yas Island.
8. Swim at Saadiyat Beach, where turtles nest and dunes meet the sea.
9. Paddle a kayak through the Eastern Mangroves or stroll the boardwalks of Jubail Mangrove Park.
10. Take a day trip to Liwa Oasis to see the towering Moreeb Dune on the edge of the Empty Quarter.
11. Take a day trip to Al Ain to walk its UNESCO-listed oasis and drive up Jebel Hafeet.
12. Sip the famous gold-flecked cappuccino at Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental.
13. Look out over the capital from the Observation Deck at 300 in Etihad Towers.
14. Lose yourself in the immersive digital art of teamLab Phenomena on Saadiyat Island.
15. Meet giant dinosaurs and meteorites at the Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi.
16. Choose your catch at Al Mina Fish Market and have it grilled on the spot.
17. Visit the Abu Dhabi Falcon Hospital to see the UAE's national bird up close.
18. Try Emirati dishes such as machboos, harees, balaleet, and luqaimat.
19. Watch craftspeople at work in the Heritage Village on the Breakwater.
20. Meet sea turtles, penguins, and dolphins at SeaWorld Yas Island.

## Sharjah

**1. Things to do** (Correct / Wrong / Not open / Don't know)

1. Explore the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization, with its golden mosaic dome and centuries of Islamic science and art.
2. Wander the restored lanes of the Heart of Sharjah heritage district.
3. Shop for spices, antiques, and incense in the shaded Souq Al Arsah, one of the oldest markets in the UAE.
4. Browse carpets, gold, and handicrafts in the tiled halls of the Central Souq, also known as the Blue Souk.
5. Join a guided visit to the elegant Al Noor Masjid on Khalid Lagoon.
6. Stroll the Al Qasba canal at dusk, when cafés spill out along the water.
7. Watch the musical fountain show at Al Majaz Waterfront.
8. Walk through falling water without getting wet at the Rain Room.
9. See contemporary art at the Sharjah Art Foundation around Al Mureijah Square.
10. Climb the restored towers of Sharjah Fort, known locally as Al Hisn.
11. Take a day trip to Mleiha to see ancient tombs, the Fossil Rock, and desert at sunset.
12. Meet sharks and rays at the Sharjah Aquarium beside the Sharjah Maritime Museum.
13. Wander the art installations and butterfly house on Al Noor Island.
14. Take a day trip to Khor Fakkan for its beach, mountains, and the cascading Al Rafisah Dam.
15. Spot kingfishers and mangroves at Kalba on the east coast.
16. Read, relax, and admire the architecture at the House of Wisdom library.
17. See Arabian leopards and oryx at the Arabia's Wildlife Centre in the Sharjah Desert Park.
18. Visit the Sharjah International Book Fair, one of the largest in the world (each November).
19. Buy fresh fish and dates at the Souq Al Jubail market.
20. Feast on shawarma, mixed grills, and kunafa at the city's Levantine restaurants.

- Where to buy: Central Souq (Blue Souk), Souq Al Arsah, Souq Al Jubail.
