# The University City Compass

**18 Student Cities · Measured Against Your Top 10 Priorities**

From Kuala Lumpur to Rabat: where each city stands on quality education, affordability, safety, growth, horizons, experience, independence, career readiness, community and lifelong opportunity. The goal is to become capable, thoughtful and adaptable, ready for life and not just a job, while helping others succeed.

## How to Read This Report

Each of the 18 cities is profiled against your ten priorities. Every priority has a score from 1 to 10 and a short note explaining it. The scale is qualitative: **10 = outstanding**, **8 = strong**, **6 = adequate**, **4 = limited**, **2 = weak**.

> Note on the scale: the original report scored out of 5. Each score here is the original score × 2, so every score is an even number. No scores were re-judged.

Scores are indicative judgements, not measurements. They are anchored to current public data (the QS World University Rankings 2026 and 2027 [1][2], the QS Arab Region Rankings 2026 [3], and the U.S. News Best Global Universities 2026 edition [4]), combined with typical tuition levels, cost of living, safety conditions and the practical realities of student life in each city as of November 2026. Different students weigh these priorities differently, so use the notes, not the numbers alone.

This report contains **no overall ranking**. Cities appear in the order they were listed, and the summary matrix at the end shows scores side by side without totals, averages or ranks.

| Priority | What it asks |
