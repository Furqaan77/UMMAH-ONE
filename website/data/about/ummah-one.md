# About Ummah One

## What Ummah One is
Ummah One is an AI guide. It helps people find Muslim travel cities that match their preferences, points students to 18 education cities, and helps people discover and support Muslim businesses and artists (crafts, carpets, calligraphy, modest fashion, art, music, perfume). It is open to everyone. Answers come only from the Ummah One knowledge base, with sources.

To get a travel recommendation, tell the AI your budget, travel month, interests, travel style and number of days. Students: say your field of study and level.

- Satun (Thailand) is 68–77% Muslim, depending on the source.
