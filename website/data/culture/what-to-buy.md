# What to Buy — best things to buy in the One Ummah cities

Part of Ummah One's art and culture guide: the best things to buy in each city and where to buy them. Taken from the One Ummah city guides; items marked (to verify) are not in the guides yet. Nothing haram is included. Gold and silk are fine to buy as gifts or for women; in Islam, men do not wear gold or silk.

## Signature buys — the 20 best things to buy

- Silk carpets: Khiva (Khiva Silk Carpet Workshop)
- Suzani embroidery, silk scarves: Samarkand (Workshops around the old town)
- Frankincense and bakhoor: Salalah (Al Haffa Souq)
- Silver and khanjars: Muscat (Mutrah Souq)
- Gold jewellery: Dubai (Gold Souk, Deira)
- Oud, spices, textiles: Doha (Souq Waqif)
- Hand-stitched bisht: Al Ahsa (Master tailors of Hofuf)
- Khalas dates: Al Ahsa (Souq Al Khamis (Thursday Market))
- Asiri honey: Abha (Local beekeepers, Tuesday Market)
- Carpets, lamps, ceramics: Istanbul (Grand Bazaar)
- Silk scarves: Bursa (Koza Han (old silk market))
- Hand-hammered copper coffee sets: Sarajevo (Kazandžiluk (coppersmiths' street))
- Silver filigree: Prizren (Old-town filigree workshops)
- Ohrid pearls: Ohrid (Old town shops)
- Lanterns, perfume oils: Cairo (Khan el-Khalili)
- Argan oil: Essaouira (Women's cooperatives)
- Batik and silver: Yogyakarta (Malioboro Street, Kotagede)
- Chikankari embroidery: Lucknow (Chowk and Aminabad)
- Lacquer bangles and pearls: Hyderabad (Laad Bazaar, Pathergatti)
- Shyrdak felt carpets: Bishkek (Local craft shops)
