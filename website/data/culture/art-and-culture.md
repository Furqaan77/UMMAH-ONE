# Art and Culture — culture, art, music, clothes, fashion, markets and handicrafts

Ummah One's art and culture guide for all 81 cities: culture, art and music; clothes, fashion and markets; and handicraft places (carpets, calligraphy, crafts, perfume). Source: Ummah One Art and Culture Guide 2026 and Ummah One team lists, November 2026. Venues, market days and workshops change: confirm locally before travelling.

## Dubai, UAE

Art and culture in Dubai:

- Culture, art and music: Museum of the Future; Global Village cultural pavilions (October to May); Alserkal Avenue (Al Quoz), a contemporary art and creative district.
- Clothes, fashion and markets: Gold Souk and Spice Souk in Deira; Textile Souk in Bur Dubai; Perfume Souk; Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood; Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates; abaya designers.
- Handicraft places: Traditional products at Global Village; textiles and tailoring shops in the Textile Souk; jewellery-making and gold work around the Gold Souk; perfume and oud at the Perfume Souk.
- Good to know: Alserkal Avenue is a former warehouse district with galleries, design, performance and cultural programmes. It complements the historic souks. Source: Visit Dubai.

## Abu Dhabi, UAE

Art and culture in Abu Dhabi:

- Culture, art and music: Louvre Abu Dhabi; teamLab Phenomena; Heritage Village.
- Clothes, fashion and markets: The Souk at Qaryat Al Beri; Souk Al Zafarana in Al Ain (day trip); Mina Market; Marina Mall.
- Handicraft places: House of Artisans at Qasr Al Hosn for talli embroidery, Al Sadu weaving and khoos palm weaving; The Souk at Qaryat Al Beri for handmade goods, carpets and traditional products.
- Good to know: Al Talli embroidery is UNESCO-listed intangible heritage of the UAE (2022). The UAE's Al Sadu is a separate listing. House of Artisans may offer scheduled workshops. Sources: UNESCO; Experience Abu Dhabi.

## Sharjah, UAE

Art and culture in Sharjah:

- Culture, art and music: Arts festivals; Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization; Sharjah Art Foundation; UNESCO World Book Capital (2019).
- Clothes, fashion and markets: Central Souq (Blue Souk); Souq Al Arsah; Souq Saqr.
- Handicraft places: Souq Al Arsah and the Heart of Sharjah for traditional crafts, textiles, antiques and souvenirs; Sharjah Heritage Museum for context on local craft traditions.
- Good to know: Sharjah was UNESCO World Book Capital in 2019, a past title. Check the Sharjah Art Foundation's exhibition calendar. Sources: UNESCO; Sharjah Art Foundation.

## Visitor tips for art, markets and crafts

How to turn a listing into a good visit:

- Before you go: check the venue's official page for opening hours, tickets, closures and photography rules. For a market, check its day and exact place; some markets move by weekday or season.
- Workshops: contact the venue or artisan cooperative directly. Ask if visitors can watch or join in, whether booking is needed, how long it takes, which languages and the fee.
- Performances: traditional music and dance are living community practices. Confirm the date and venue, and prefer community-run or institution-run programmes.
- Buying crafts: ask who made it, where, with which materials and technique, and about care and shipping. Distinguish local handmade craft from imported or mass-produced souvenirs. For antiques, carpets, jewellery or protected objects, ask for receipts and export papers.
- Photos: ask before photographing artisans, performers, residents or places of worship. Respect restricted areas and local dress and conduct.
- Access and families: confirm step-free access, child suitability, restrooms, heat and transport directly with the venue.
