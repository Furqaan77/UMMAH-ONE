# Art and Culture — culture, art, music, clothes, fashion, markets and handicrafts

Ummah One's art and culture guide: where to find culture, art and music; clothes, fashion and markets; and handicrafts (carpets, calligraphy, crafts, perfume) in the One Ummah cities. Source: Ummah One team lists, 12 Nov 2026. To verify.

## Culture art and music — 13 cities

- Konya, Türkiye: Rumi, whirling dervish ceremonies
- Istanbul, Türkiye: museums, calligraphy, music
- Doha, Qatar: Museum of Islamic Art
- Abu Dhabi, UAE: Louvre Abu Dhabi
- Sharjah, UAE: arts festivals, museums
- Essaouira, Morocco: Gnawa music festival
- Marrakesh, Morocco: storytellers, Jemaa el-Fnaa square
- Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina: film festival, Bosnian music
- Yogyakarta, Indonesia: batik, gamelan, puppet theatre
- Lucknow, India: Urdu poetry, Kathak dance
- Kazan, Russia: Tatar culture
- Baku, Azerbaijan: mugham music, carpet museum
- Cairo, Egypt: Arabic music, literature

## Clothes fashion and markets — 11 cities

- Istanbul, Türkiye: Grand Bazaar, modest fashion brands
- Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia: modest fashion
- Dubai, UAE: abaya designers, souks, malls
- Doha, Qatar: Souq Waqif
- Bursa, Türkiye: silk
- Yogyakarta, Indonesia: batik clothing
- Lucknow, India: Chikankari embroidery
- Hyderabad, India: pearls, Laad Bazaar bangles
- Marrakesh, Morocco: souks, kaftans
- Cairo, Egypt: Khan el-Khalili bazaar
- Tashkent, Uzbekistan: Chorsu Bazaar

## Handicrafts — where to find each craft

- Carpets: Konya and Istanbul (Türkiye), Baku (Azerbaijan), Bukhara (Uzbekistan), Marrakesh (Morocco)
- Calligraphy: Istanbul (Türkiye)
- Silk and embroidery (suzani): Bukhara and Samarkand (Uzbekistan)
- Wood carving: Khiva (Uzbekistan), Essaouira (Morocco), Bali (Indonesia), Zanzibar (Tanzania, carved doors)
- Copperwork: Sarajevo and Mostar (Bosnia and Herzegovina)
- Silver filigree: Prizren (Kosovo)
- Silver and batik: Yogyakarta (Indonesia)
- Pottery: Djerba (Tunisia)
- Leather: Marrakesh (Morocco)
- Bidriware metalwork: Hyderabad (India)
- Perfume and oud: Dubai (UAE)
- Frankincense: Muscat and Salalah (Oman)
