# Travel Convenience — how easy it is to visit places in each city

Part of Ummah One's travel guide (the Ummah One team's "Travel Convenience Guide", research edition, 13 Nov 2026). For each city, the places to visit are listed from easiest to hardest, with an ease-of-visit score out of 10. The score is an estimate of how easy it is to get there and walk around (transport, roads, transfer time, stairs, terrain, heat). It is not a score for beauty or importance, and it does not mean wheelchair access. Scores are estimates, to verify. Always check opening days, tickets and transport before going.

Score meaning: 9–10 very easy · 8–8.9 easy · 7–7.9 fairly easy (a transfer or some walking) · 6–6.9 needs planning (car, boat or effort) · 5–5.9 hard or remote · below 5 very hard (long trips, hiking).
