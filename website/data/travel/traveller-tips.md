# Traveller tips: from people who visited, checked by the Ummah One team

Tips written by travellers who visited these cities, in the "Help check the guide" questionnaire. Approved by the Ummah One team in November 2026.

## Almaty, Kazakhstan

- Visit the Alma-Arasan hot spring.
- Try Kazakh food.
- Take a horse ride in the mountains at Talgar.
- Almaty is a good place to visit if you like hiking, mountain climbing and nature.
- Shopping: you can find these things not only at the Green Bazaar or on Zhibek Zholy. Talk to locals and they can point you to other markets (bazaars) with many more things. You can also find sellers on Instagram.

## Abu Dhabi, UAE

- Mangroves: if you only have time for one, choose Jubail Mangrove Park. Walk the boardwalk, then take a guided kayak tour. Allow 2–3 hours. Good for people who enjoy nature, the outdoors and photography.
- Day trip to Liwa Oasis and Moreeb Dune: start early (about 7 am), carry water and snacks, and fill up with fuel before the remote desert. A suggested day: the date-palm farms and old settlements of Liwa Oasis in the late morning, Moreeb Dune around midday, lunch in Liwa, a guided 4x4 ride, dune trip or sandboarding in the afternoon, sunset over the Empty Quarter, then the drive back.
- Day trip to Al Ain from Dubai, suggested times: leave Dubai at 7:30 am; walk through Al Ain Oasis 9:30–11 am; Al Jahili Fort 11:15 am–12:15 pm; lunch 12:30–1:30 pm; Jebel Hafeet Beehive Tombs 2–2:45 pm; Green Mubazzarah 3–3:45 pm; drive up Jebel Hafeet for the views 4–5:30 pm; then return to Dubai.
- Gold cappuccino: served in the grand lobby of Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental. It is espresso with steamed milk and edible gold flakes.
- Observation Deck at 300 (Etihad Towers): go at 4–5 pm for daylight views. Sunset is best for photos, and after sunset you see the lit-up skyline and Corniche. Allow 45–90 minutes.
- teamLab Phenomena: combine it with the nearby Louvre Abu Dhabi for a half-day or full-day visit.
- Natural History Museum: visit it and teamLab Phenomena on the same day. One is about the natural world, the other is digital art.
- Al Mina Fish Market: buy fresh prawns and a whole local fish, and have them grilled with garlic and Gulf spices at one of the market restaurants. Check the cooking and service charges before you hand over your seafood.
- Emirati food is worth trying, but there is no need for a separate trip just for it. Try machboos, balaleet and luqaimat, and a small portion of harees if you are curious.
- Heritage Village: a good 30–60 minute stop if you are already near Marina Mall or the Corniche, and entry is free. It is not worth a special trip, especially when the craftspeople are not working. For Emirati history and crafts, Qasr Al Hosn and the House of Artisans are better.
- SeaWorld Yas Island: good if you mainly want to see sea turtles, penguins and dolphins, but it is expensive. You may want to skip it if you are on a budget, have only one day in Abu Dhabi, or do not like seeing sea animals kept in captivity.
- Yas Island has a heritage and pearl-diving experience where you can catch your own pearl and have jewellery made from it.

## Dubai, UAE

- A traveller reported in November 2026 that La Mer is closed (to verify).
- Travellers also suggest Jebel Jais, water sports and Al Seef. Jebel Jais is in Ras Al Khaimah, outside Dubai (to verify).
- Global Village is worth a visit if you are there in season.
- Dubai is an international hub more than a centre of Emirati culture: you can find food from all over the world, and shopping is not limited to the things listed in this guide.

## Istanbul, Türkiye

- Basilica Cistern: a different experience from Istanbul's mosques, palaces and museums. Worth it for a first visit if the ticket fits your budget, but see Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, Süleymaniye Mosque and Topkapı Palace first.
- Grand Bazaar: historic, beautiful and fun to explore, but very commercial, with high tourist prices. Spend 60–90 minutes walking its old lanes, looking at the craftsmanship and browsing the shops.
- Spice Bazaar: not worth a special trip across the city on its own. It is worthwhile together with the Rüstem Pasha Mosque, the old shopping streets around it and the Eminönü waterfront.
- Ferry to Kadıköy: the ferry ride and the neighbourhood together are better than the food market alone. After the historic sights of Sultanahmet, Kadıköy is a refreshing change.
- Leather goods: a reviewer rated Istanbul 8.5/10 for buying leather goods, but only 6.5/10 for bargains.
- Take time to read about the history of the artefacts you see. A traveller said it made them feel closer to the Prophet (peace be upon him) and his companions.

## Sharjah, UAE

- A traveller said Sharjah is the most cultural emirate of the UAE. Several smaller organisations there also share the culture and products of other countries.
