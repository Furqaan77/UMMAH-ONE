# Prompt: Ummah One — Product Plan (max 5 pages)

## Your role
You are a product strategist. Use the BMAD Method Analyst workflow (product brief). Write a product plan for **Ummah One**, an AI guide built on RAG (retrieval-augmented generation).

## Writing rules
- **Maximum 5 pages.** Simple English. Short sentences. Mostly tables and bullets.
- No long explanations or padding.
- Do not plan revenue or earnings. Money is not a goal right now.
- Do not invent facts. Mark estimates as "estimate". Mark unchecked facts as "to verify".
- Use only the decisions below. Do not add features the decisions exclude.

## Mission
Help grow the Muslim economy **3X by 2034** (7 years from launch in 2027), measured as the **total GDP of OIC Muslim countries**. Grow these areas: travel and tourism, education, culture, art, food and snacks, clothes and fashion, heritage, music, palaces, and Muslim businesses.
- 3X is the mission. Ummah One does not claim a share of it; it tracks its own numbers beside it.
- Note that 3X in 7 years requires about 17% growth every year.

## What Ummah One is
One AI engine (RAG) that powers the Ummah One app and can also run as a standalone product. It encourages well-to-do Muslims to travel to the 87 Muslim cities below, based on their preferences. It points students to the 18 education cities. It helps people discover and support Muslim businesses and artists.
- Open to everyone, including non-Muslims. Under-18s have full access.
- Channels: mobile app, website, WhatsApp bot.
- Languages at launch: English, Arabic. Voice input later.

## Users, in priority order
1. Muslim businesses: crafts, carpets and calligraphy; modest fashion; art and artists; music and performers; perfume
2. Contributors: people who add places and content (local guides, artists, businesses, travellers)
3. Families and travellers: all groups (families, young professionals, couples, retirees, students). Women-traveller features from day one
4. Students: from everywhere

Well-wishers are respected community members who recommend places (elders, imams, mosque members).

## Product decisions
**City pages (87 cities)** include: halal food; safety; budget; best season; family-friendly and women-friendly ratings; heritage, palaces and museums; culture, art and music; local food and snacks; clothes, fashion and markets; Muslim businesses to support; wheelchair and elderly access; events and festivals calendar; safety disclaimer ("check official travel advice") on every page.
- **Excluded:** mosques and prayer times, visa rules, prices, maps, day-by-day itineraries, group trips, Umrah add-ons, online shop links.
- Bali, Kotor, Budva, Podgorica, Ohrid get a "halal-friendliness" label.
- Srinagar, Grozny, Derbent, Makhachkala, Erbil get a safety section with links to official travel advisories.

**Business and artist profiles:** free listing for now; location and contact only; trust grows through verified customer reviews; owners can reply to reviews; individual artists and craftspeople get profiles. Music: all music by Muslim artists is allowed.

**Education pages (18 cities):** all good universities in those cities; undergraduate, master's/PhD, medicine, short courses; estimated tuition and living costs; information only. No scholarships and no application help. No proof of study needed to review.

**Recommendations:**
- The AI asks: budget, travel month, interests, travel style, number of days; for students, field of study and level.
- Ranking order: interests and budget, then safety, then heritage and culture, then halal-friendliness.
- Every recommendation shows **why** it was made and its **sources**.

**Feedback and trust:**
- Login: phone OTP. Travel reviews need a photo taken at the place.
- Anonymous reviews are allowed (still verified).
- Contributors and well-wishers earn badges and levels.
- **The Ummah One team can override anything; user feedback comes second.** The team settles disputes.
- No sectarian or political content; the team removes it.

**RAG and AI:**
- Claude on the cloud first, then Qwen or Llama on our own servers. Keep the design model-agnostic.
- The AI answers only from the knowledge base, with sources.
- Allowed sources: official tourism websites, UNESCO, university websites, QS/THE, team research, verified user reviews and photos, contributor and well-wisher content. **No Wikipedia.**
- Content is updated when a change is reported.
- AI hosting budget: under $50,000 in year 1. Built from scratch with BMAD and Claude Code.

## Promotion: mosques only
- No partners other than mosques (no universities, tourism boards, airlines or halal bodies).
- Promote travel in mosques where average family income is above **$60,000**. Leadership decides which mosques qualify.
- Countries: USA, UK, Canada, Western Europe, and high-income areas of Pakistan, Malaysia and Indonesia. Add Singapore, where contacts exist.
- Existing contacts: Los Angeles, London, Chicago, Michigan, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Kansas.
- Year-1 target: 50 mosques.
- Outreach: video calls, own network, and elders who know the imams.
- In-mosque methods: QR posters on notice boards; the mosque's WhatsApp group or newsletter; separate messages for women's and youth groups.
- No scholar approval required. Mosques get nothing extra. Nothing is sold inside the prayer hall.

## Team and timeline
- 30 volunteers: travel and tourism experts plus engineers. The product lead is the person who answered the questions.
- First version in **3 months** (early February 2027). Company not yet registered. Minimal GDPR (consent, privacy policy, data deletion).
